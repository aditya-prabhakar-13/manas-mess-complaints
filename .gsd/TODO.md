## Format


## Priority Levels

| Level | Use For |
|-------|---------|
| `high` 🔴 | Blocking issues, urgent fixes |
| `medium` 🟡 | Normal priority (default) |
| `low` 🟢 | Nice-to-have, future ideas |
